leetsay is free software, provided "as is", without warranty of any kind.

- leetsay types what you dictate into whichever window or application is
  active. It does not check which program, chat, form or document that is, or
  what that program does with the text. Check where your cursor is before you
  speak.
- Speech recognition makes mistakes. Read the text before you send or submit
  it. Do not rely on leetsay where a wrong, missing or misplaced word could
  cause harm or loss, such as medical, legal, financial, safety or emergency
  use.
- If you set leetsay to press a key for you after dictating (such as Enter),
  that key can send a message or submit a form straight away.
