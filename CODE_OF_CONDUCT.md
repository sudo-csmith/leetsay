# Code of conduct

leetsay's issues and discussions are for making the app better. To keep them
useful and welcoming:

- Be respectful and patient, with the maintainer and with each other.
- Keep to the topic: the app, its bugs and ideas for it.
- Disagree with ideas, not with people.
- No harassment, insults, personal attacks or discrimination of any kind.
- No spam, advertising or off-topic links.
- Don't share anyone's private information, including your own: logs and
  screenshots should not show names, email addresses or other personal details.

## If something goes wrong

To report a problem with someone's behaviour,
[open a conduct report](https://github.com/sudo-csmith/leetsay/issues/new?template=conduct_report.yml).
It only asks for a private way to talk: please don't put the details in the
public report.

## What happens

The maintainer may edit, hide or remove comments, close or lock issues, and
block accounts that don't follow this code. Reports are handled as privately
as possible.
